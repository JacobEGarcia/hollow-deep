HOLLOW DEEP

THE OFFICIAL GUIDE · RECOVERED VOL. I OF ?

I. ON THE DESCENT. You have found the stair that does not appear on any map. Below it waits the Deep, and at its root the Gate of the Hollow God, shut by three seals of bone.

II. ON THE HOLLOWED. The ones who stayed. They walk slowly because they no longer remember why they walk. Do not let them remember you. A blade will return them to the dark — two good blows, sometimes three.

III. ON THE SEALS. Three idols of bone lie in the far rooms. Carry all three to the Gate. The guide is emphatic: do not look at the idols longer than necessary.
